# Data

Benchmark subsets live here. They are **gitignored** — large files and frozen
splits shouldn't churn in the repo — with one explicit exception:
`miniset.jsonl`, the hand-written Phase-2 smoke set, is tracked. What's
committed otherwise: the revision pin, the checksums, and the license/terms
verdict.

## Sources

1. **xLAM-60k** (Salesforce) — 60,000 tool-calling entries, 3,605 distinct
   tool names in the pinned revision, 21 API categories. The categories are
   the paper's descriptive taxonomy only; the **split and cluster unit is the
   tool**. Load with
   `datasets.load_dataset("Salesforce/xlam-function-calling-60k", revision="<pinned-hash>")`.
   Access granted 2026-10-23 (click-through; gated: auto).
2. **Hand-written mini-set** (`miniset.jsonl`, ~20 tasks) — pipeline smoke
   test and multi-step seed. Excluded from headline numbers.
3. **Hand-written multi-step set** (`multistep.jsonl`, ~30 tasks, each with
   deterministic mock tools) — the judge's territory.

The xLAM-7b-fc-r checkpoint and the cost-aware-router outcomes were dropped
from the design in the 2026-10-23 grill (see `docs/EVAL_DESIGN.md`).

## License / republication verdict (drafted 2026-10-26; pending owner sign-off)

**Status: DRAFT — not a verdict.** Owner decision (2026-10-26): the
**publication rule is approved; the license verdict is not**. Until the owner
confirms the verdict, do **not** publish raw dataset rows, queries, gold
answers, full tool schemas, or traces that reproduce them. The draft below
stays a draft.

**Public card facts.** The Hub license tag reads **`cc-by-4.0`**. Access is
gated: the card says "Acknowledge to follow corresponding license and cite
APIGen to access the repository", and files require accepting conditions while
logged in. The card asks users to cite APIGen (Liu et al., 2024,
arXiv:2406.18518). It also says the release is "for research purposes only in
support of an academic paper" and points to Salesforce's AUP and AI AUP.

**Draft verdict.** CC-BY-4.0, if it governs the data file, permits sharing and
adaptation (including commercial use) with attribution, a license link, and an
indication of changes. The gating/citation language is compatible with
attribution but may add conditions. The "research purposes only" sentence
reads as a use limitation and is in tension with CC-BY-4.0; the owner's
accepted terms must resolve that before any redistribution.

**May be published now (no raw dataset content):** aggregates, counts, tool
names, revision hashes, filter definitions, and hand-written tasks.

**Do not publish yet:** raw rows, queries, gold answers, full tool schemas, or
model traces that reproduce them. A trace containing a query, an answer, or a
full schema is treated as dataset content for licensing purposes.

**After owner confirmation**, a sampled trace may be published only with:
attribution to Salesforce/APIGen plus the citation, a CC-BY-4.0 license link, a
"changes made" note, and compliance with any additional click-through terms.
If the accepted terms impose research-only or no-redistribution conditions,
publish links and aggregates only.

**Card/data discrepancy and interim filter (owner decision, 2026-10-26).**
The public card describes a `required` boolean on each parameter, but the
pinned revision's 362,482 parameter descriptors contain only `type`,
`description`, and (optionally) `default`: **zero** have a `required` key. The
Phase-0 required-argument filter therefore treats a parameter without a
`default` as required — but that is an **interim assumption/heuristic, not the
dataset's rule**. The final rule is pending the audit in
`docs/REQUIRED_FILTER_AUDIT.md` and owner sign-off before pre-registration.
The discrepancy stays in the record either way.

## Splits

Split by **tool**, never randomly. A held-out tool never appears as a
gold/answer tool in dev. Candidate tools **may** appear as distractors in a
dev row's `tools` list; that exposure is counted per candidate and disclosed,
and it does **not** trigger replacement. The frozen candidate order, seed,
sha256, eligibility filters, and per-candidate counts are in
`docs/CANDIDATES.md`; rerun `scripts/select_candidates.py` to reproduce them.

Invalid-row filters (EVAL_DESIGN §5) are applied **before** the split
freezes: answer-tools only, bad/missing answer names, empty answers, malformed
gold calls (missing/non-string name, non-object/string-encoded arguments;
excluded and counted, zero on the pinned revision), missing required arguments
under the **interim** heuristic (final rule pending
`docs/REQUIRED_FILTER_AUDIT.md`), and mixed candidate+non-candidate cases
(excluded and counted). Phase 0 on the pinned revision found 71 eligible tools
with at least 100 single-tool cases under the structural filters; the interim
required-arguments heuristic gives a diagnostic 63-tool all-filter pool. All
12 frozen candidates clear the floor after every filter.
